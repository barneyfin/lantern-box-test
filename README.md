# Lantern Box tests

Test builds of **Gloam Hallow** and the Lantern Box kit's test page, for trying on any device. Not finished, and not for sharing.

Open https://barneyfin.github.io/lantern-box-test/ and choose:

- **Gloam Hallow** (`gloam-hallow.html`): the game, with its Testing screen in Settings. Since kit 0.21.0 a run is saved at each lantern: close the tab mid-run and Continue is on the title (Testing's "Pause closes the page" does the same as closing it). Since kit 0.24.0: a life for each boss up to five (Testing's "Lives at the start" helps reach five), The Moor Mother closing in, and the lightning bolt drawn wider. Since kit 0.25.0: graves and other props meet the ground on slopes, and the boss's name plaque grows to fit on a phone. Since kit 0.28.0: the game starts before its soundtrack has arrived, which comes at the end of the file and fades in; since 0.28.1 it is unpacked a track at a time, so play doesn't stutter as it arrives. Since kit 0.27.0: the climb (each place from Blood Red Rain on lighter before its halfway lantern and busier after it) and, on a phone, pull the stick down to the side to roll (design system 21). Since kit 0.31.0: The Endless Rot, a new mode of waves in arenas (design system 23), opened by beating The Veiled Ghost or from Testing's "The Endless Rot" switch, and a Personal bests tab in High scores. Since kit 0.32.0, from wave 4 some crawlers and ghouls rise up through cracked ember warnings inside the arena. Since 0.32.0-r1, any monster there may drop a sword or spell upgrade, about 1 in 30. Since 0.32.0-r2, it gets harder every wave, and health only half refills between waves. Since 0.32.0-r3 (tuning 3), mana doesn't refill between waves at all. Since 0.32.0-r4 (tuning 4), the heal costs half the mana bar there; and everywhere, a walker standing higher than you on a slope sweeps low. Since kit 0.33.0, on a phone, putting your thumb down to turn never rolls you (no flick in the first 0.3 s, nothing pulled down at the bottom edge, no double tap), and a roll out of a sword swing goes the way you push. Since 0.33.0-r1 (tuning 5), a walker even a little higher than you on a slope sweeps low, and in The Endless Rot its blow is heavier (35% of your health before the waves' ramp, was 25%). Since kit 0.35.0, the difficulty setting is in the game: once something is bought at the Echo Shrine (or The Endless Rot is open), Start game shows the choice of run with Shallow, Grim and Agony under the runs. Shallow is the journey as it was, Grim makes monsters hit harder and take more hits place by place from Blood Red Rain on, Agony more still, and the echo kept scales with it. The Bare Reckoning is always Grim, and The Endless Rot has its own three. The setting shows as a tag on the pause screen, the run's end and the win; three losses in a row bring a suggestion on the run's end. The first time a device opens this version its high scores, personal bests and any saved run are wiped once (the stolen echo and everything bought stay). Testing's Difficulty row is gone. On a phone the first run's "Pull down to the side to roll" hint goes once you have rolled, and the "Jump" and "Cast" hints no longer land off the screen. Since 0.35.0-r1, Grim and Agony are harder on the journey in two more ways (Shallow and The Endless Rot are unchanged; The Bare Reckoning has Grim's). Less is found: an ordinary monster drops something 1 time in 7 on Grim and 1 in 10 on Agony (it was about 1 in 4), a walker 1 in 4 and 1 in 5 (it was about 43%), and each place holds about 2 fewer health potions on Grim and 3 fewer on Agony. And monsters get 3% faster with each place, moving and attacking, carrying on into the second loop on top of its own speed. After three losses in a row on Grim in Night, the run's end now offers Shallow. Since 0.35.0-r2, the heal spell costs more mana on the journey: half the bar on Grim and 60% on Agony (30% on Shallow, as before; The Bare Reckoning has Grim's; The Endless Rot keeps its own). Since kit 0.36.0, the bosses fight back two new ways (design system 26). Stand at a boss's feet too long and it knocks you back: a steady ember glow spreads round it for 0.7 s, then a burst throws you away from it, further on harder settings and at later bosses (get out of the glow to dodge it). And at marks on its bar it mends: it goes still in the mana bar's colour, your sword glances off, and only an attack spell that lands stops it. The Veiled Ghost does neither on Shallow or Grim in the first loop; the other bosses do on every setting. The first time a device opens this version its high scores, The Echo Infusion's best and any saved run are cleared once (The Endless Rot's bests, the stolen echo and everything bought stay). Since 0.36.0-r1, Night has health potions of its own lying on its path: on Grim (and so The Bare Reckoning) one at the start and one at the boss's door, on Shallow the door's only, on Agony neither, in both loops. Since 0.36.0-r2, Night's own potions are in the first loop only and lie there again after a life lost (each ahead of the lantern you come back at), and the bosses mend more: from one mend of 10% to three of half the bar, by boss, setting and loop (each mark on the bar still mends once a fight). Since kit 0.45.2: The Echo Shrine's arrow keys reach every button, ones that can't be bought yet too (greyed, and Enter does nothing there); on Spells and potions left and right go across between the two columns, and only the page's tab turns the page; after Choose the selection stays where it was. Since 0.45.1-r1: nothing changes on this link. The game's file is the playtest's too, and in the playtest's copy the Cosmetics page, the look at Picture: Fastest and the repaint are now on; here they are still switched in Settings, Testing. Since kit 0.45.1: one change to words. On the win's card, the button that starts the second loop reads "Go round again (harder)" (it was "Go round again"); the line above it, Finish and the key hint keep their words. Since 0.45.0-r1: two changes to words. In the choice of run the row under the runs reads "Difficulty:", whichever run is chosen (it was the run's name); and the Echo Shrine's footer is its sentence and the Back button, with no key hint (Backspace still goes back). Since kit 0.45.0: the game can paint its backgrounds again after the browser resets its graphics (the picture going black, or its layers missing, for the rest of a visit: the Fire, 22 November). It is off here until you switch it on in Settings, Testing: "Repaint after a reset" On; the CrazyGames build has it on. With it on, a play check whose result says "the picture was lost" should show its backgrounds back within a second or two. Since kit 0.44.0: on the Echo Shrine's Cosmetics page (Settings, Testing: "Cosmetics page" on), a glow button's dot and a glowing belt's band are in the strong glow's colours, the glow the game draws. A line now shows at the right of "Lantern glass" while a bought look is not drawn: "With Effects off, glows and light are not drawn" with Effects off in Settings, or "Glows and light show at Picture: Faster or Best" with Picture at Fastest while "Looks at Fastest" is Off. The CrazyGames build now keeps the look at Fastest; here that is still Testing's "Looks at Fastest": On, and with it on there is no line at Fastest. Since kit 0.43.0: a bought look can be kept at Picture: Fastest, where a slow device plays and every glow is off. It is off until you switch it on in Settings, Testing: "Looks at Fastest" On, with "Cosmetics page" on as well. A glowing belt then keeps its glow as two plain lines, and the wanderer keeps the light round his lantern and at his feet. "On, lights laid over" is the fallback, with the coloured lights duller. To see it, set Settings, Picture to Fastest. "A belt's glow" now starts at Strong. Since kit 0.42.0: the Echo Shrine's Cosmetics page, to try. It is off until you switch it on in Settings, Testing: "Cosmetics page" On (buy with your echo: a glow for a belt you own at 450, lantern glass in four colours at 300) or On, all bought (try every glow of a belt you own and every glass; none of it is saved). "A belt's glow" picks Soft or Strong. With it Off the shrine is as it was. Since 0.36.0-r4, on Agony Night's monsters hit and take blows as on Grim in the first loop (a crawler's or a flyer's hit 10% and a mouth-hat's 15%; a crawler falls to 2 blows, a flyer to 1 and a mouth-hat to 3), so Agony's harder monsters start at Blood Red Rain; the second loop's Night is as it was. Since 0.36.0-r3, Night's own potions are on Agony too (at the start and at the boss's door, as on Grim), and Personal bests shows The Echo Infusion as it shows The Endless Rot: a best for each setting played, under the run's name (the one best kept before no longer shows). Since kit 0.34.3, at Picture Fastest an attacking walker or ghoul stands in a set stance so its attack's pictures are shared again. Since kit 0.34.2, at Picture Fastest the walker's and the ghoul's legs move in steps and their pictures are kept, for slow tablets. Since kit 0.34.1, a crawler's lunge or a flyer's dive no longer turns you round, and the number in "Wave 4" stands as tall as the word. Since kit 0.34.0, the walker and the ghoul walk with their feet on the ground, and the walker's high sweep (arms up) stoops down to your head and shoulders: roll under it. Since kit 0.33.1, the Mist's leaves drift smoothly however long you've played, and on a computer the ; key beside L rolls as Shift does (Shift can open Windows' Sticky Keys box).
