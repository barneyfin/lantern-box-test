# Lantern Box tests

Test builds of **Gloam Hallow** and the Lantern Box kit's test page, for trying on any device. Not finished, and not for sharing.

Open https://barneyfin.github.io/lantern-box-test/ and choose:

- **Gloam Hallow** (`gloam-hallow.html`): the game, with its Testing screen in Settings. Since kit 0.21.0 a run is saved at each lantern: close the tab mid-run and Continue is on the title (Testing's "Pause closes the page" does the same as closing it). Since kit 0.24.0: a life for each boss up to five (Testing's "Lives at the start" helps reach five), The Moor Mother closing in, and the lightning bolt drawn wider. Since kit 0.25.0: graves and other props meet the ground on slopes, and the boss's name plaque grows to fit on a phone. Since kit 0.28.0: the game starts before its soundtrack has arrived, which comes at the end of the file and fades in; since 0.28.1 it is unpacked a track at a time, so play doesn't stutter as it arrives. Since kit 0.27.0: the climb (each place from Blood Red Rain on lighter before its halfway lantern and busier after it) and, on a phone, pull the stick down to the side to roll (design system 21). Since kit 0.31.0: The Endless Rot, a new mode of waves in arenas (design system 23), opened by beating The Veiled Ghost or from Testing's "The Endless Rot" switch, and a Personal bests tab in High scores. Since kit 0.32.0, from wave 4 some crawlers and ghouls rise up through cracked ember warnings inside the arena. Since 0.32.0-r1, any monster there may drop a sword or spell upgrade, about 1 in 30. Since 0.32.0-r2, it gets harder every wave, and health only half refills between waves. Since 0.32.0-r3 (tuning 3), mana doesn't refill between waves at all. Since 0.32.0-r4 (tuning 4), the heal costs half the mana bar there; and everywhere, a walker standing higher than you on a slope sweeps low. Since kit 0.33.0, on a phone, putting your thumb down to turn never rolls you (no flick in the first 0.3 s, nothing pulled down at the bottom edge, no double tap), and a roll out of a sword swing goes the way you push. Since 0.33.0-r1 (tuning 5), a walker even a little higher than you on a slope sweeps low, and in The Endless Rot its blow is heavier (35% of your health before the waves' ramp, was 25%). Since kit 0.34.2, at Picture Fastest the walker's and the ghoul's legs move in steps and their pictures are kept, for slow tablets. Since kit 0.34.1, a crawler's lunge or a flyer's dive no longer turns you round, and the number in "Wave 4" stands as tall as the word. Since kit 0.34.0, the walker and the ghoul walk with their feet on the ground, and the walker's high sweep (arms up) stoops down to your head and shoulders: roll under it. Since kit 0.33.1, the Mist's leaves drift smoothly however long you've played, and on a computer the ; key beside L rolls as Shift does (Shift can open Windows' Sticky Keys box).
- **Play check** (`gloam-hallow.html?check`): Start game plays by itself for about two minutes, through all four places and their bosses, and gives a result to copy.
- **Play check: The Endless Rot** (`gloam-hallow.html?check=rot`, since 0.31.0-r1): the same for the new mode, in its four arenas at their busiest waves, the first time round and again, harder. Since 0.31.0-r2 the picture settles while the first wave fills Night's arena, as play begins.
- **Trials for slow devices** (kit 0.20.2): the play check with one look trade-off switched on from the address (`gloam-hallow.html?check&trial=cloud`, `boss12`, `lines540`, `lines432`, `few`, `nohud`, `hero` (kit 0.20.4), `mon` (kit 0.20.5), or several joined by commas), to time each on a slow device before anything is decided.
- **Your runs** (`gloam-hallow.html?runs`, since 0.32.0-r2): the run log, kept for runs played from this site, the last 20, to send or copy; The Endless Rot's runs say the waves reached and when, each life lost, the Graces, lives and upgrades.
- **Test page** (`lb-sampler.html`): every drawing part and screen, and the speed check (tap Speed check, or press T).

On an iPhone, for full screen: open Gloam Hallow, then Share, then Add to Home Screen, and play it from its icon.

Both files are built single-file pages (made by `python lantern-box/kit/build.py` in Barney's arcade). The fonts embedded in them, IM Fell English and Alegreya Sans, are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; their licences are in `licences/`. The music was made with Google Gemini.
